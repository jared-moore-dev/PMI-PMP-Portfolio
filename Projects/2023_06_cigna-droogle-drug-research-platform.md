## PROJECT OBJECTIVE & OUTCOME

**Objective:**
Cigna's contracting teams negotiate directly with drug manufacturers, and they were going into those conversations without good visibility into what competitors were paying. Droogle was built to close that gap. The platform focused on two things: matching branded drugs to biosimilar alternatives, and modeling competitor discount rates using public data so our teams had a realistic benchmark before sitting down at the table.

**Outcome:**
We delivered a working digital dashboard that integrated public drug pricing data, biosimilar matching logic, and estimated competitor discount modeling. The project was recognized for its innovative use of public data to address a real business problem and for the quality of the final presentation. We won our prompt category at the 2023 TECDP Summer Innovation Project Final Presentations on August 10, 2023, placing first among teams competing within the pharmacy/contracting prompt. We did not win the overall competition across all three prompts.

## LESSONS LEARNED

1. Never build on data you have not validated. We assumed the public pricing data existed, was accessible, and was structured in a way we could use. It was not — at least not the way we expected. Adding a data discovery spike in Sprint 1 before any design work locked in assumptions saved us from a much more painful mid-project reset.
2. What stakeholders ask for and what they actually need are usually different things. The contracting team's initial requests were surface-level. Structured discovery and iterative sprint reviews kept pulling us closer to the real problem. That is the Product Owner's job.
3. Scope conversations go better when you lead with the constraint, not the refusal. Telling stakeholders we could not build something went nowhere. Showing them the data boundaries transparently and co-designing something that still solved the core problem went much further.

## PMI APPLICATION NARRATIVE

As Product Owner for Cigna's 2023 Summer Innovation Program, I led stakeholder engagement and requirements management for Droogle, a drug research and contracting intelligence platform built to give Cigna's pharmacy contracting teams better footing heading into manufacturer negotiations. The platform focused on two capabilities: matching branded drugs to biosimilar alternatives and benchmarking likely competitor discount rates using publicly available pricing data.

My role as Product Owner was to stay close to the business stakeholders and make sure what we built actually solved their problem. I started with structured discovery sessions with our project sponsor and contracting domain experts to understand how negotiations worked, what information they were missing, and where the gaps were costing them leverage. I turned those findings into a prioritized backlog with user stories and acceptance criteria the development team could build against.

The biggest challenge of the project came early. In planning, I realized that the public drug pricing data we intended to build on had not been validated for accessibility or structure. That was a real risk: if we designed around data we could not actually get, we would be rewriting the plan mid-build. I escalated it to the PM and sponsor, and we added a data discovery spike to Sprint 1. That decision let us scope the data layer around sources we had confirmed before any design work locked in assumptions we could not support.

Throughout the sprint cycle I kept the stakeholder relationship active. I ran check-ins with the sponsor and mentor, presented completed features at sprint reviews, and brought feedback back into backlog refinement each sprint. A recurring challenge was managing expectations around what public data could support. Contracting team members would request features that assumed pricing detail we could not access. Rather than shutting those conversations down, I walked them through the data constraints directly and worked with them to find alternatives that addressed the core need within what was actually available.

The project closed with a formal executive presentation on August 10, 2023. Droogle won its prompt category, placing first among all teams in the pharmacy contracting challenge.
